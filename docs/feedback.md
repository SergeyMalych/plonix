# Report a bug or suggest a feature

Something doesn't work, or you wish Plonix did something it doesn't? Tell us on GitHub. Reports and ideas from people using Plonix decide what gets fixed and built next.

## From the app

- **Feedback**, at the foot of the sidebar, opens a small menu:
  - **Report a bug** opens a new bug report on [github.com/SergeyMalych/plonix](https://github.com/SergeyMalych/plonix/issues) in your browser.
  - **Suggest a feature** opens a new feature request.
  - **See what others reported** opens the list of open issues, so you can add to one that is already there.
- **Help › Report a Bug…** and **Help › Suggest a Feature…** in the menu bar do the same from any window, the Start screen included.

The form opens with only your Plonix version and your system (such as *macOS 15.1 (Apple silicon)* or *Windows 11 (build 22631, x86_64)*) filled in. Nothing from your projects goes with it: no traffic, hosts, cookies or tokens. You write the rest, and nothing is sent until you press **Submit** on GitHub. You need a GitHub account to submit.

## Without the app

Open [a new issue](https://github.com/SergeyMalych/plonix/issues/new/choose) and pick **Bug report** or **Feature request**. For a bug, `plonix --version` prints the version to paste in.

## A good report

- **What you did, what you expected and what happened.** Steps someone else can follow are the fastest way to a fix.
- **Leave out secrets.** Remove tokens, cookies, passwords and the names of sites you test from anything you paste.
- **If Plonix crashed**, the next launch offers to open a report with the details filled in. See [Crash reports](crash-reports.md).
- **A security problem in Plonix itself** goes privately, not in a public issue: see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/SergeyMalych/plonix/security/policy).
