## Only test what you are allowed to test

Use Plonix only on systems you own, or that you have clear permission to test. Intercepting, replaying, scanning or changing traffic to someone else's system without permission can be illegal. Scope in Plonix helps you stay inside your target, but it is not permission.

## Your data stays on your computer

Captured traffic, projects, findings and settings are stored only on your computer, in folders you choose. Plonix does not upload them anywhere. Things that reach the internet are things you start: your own requests, the Market catalog, update checks, and the AI agents you connect.

## Anonymous usage statistics

If you allow it, Plonix sends anonymous usage statistics at most once a day: a random install id, the Plonix version, your operating system and CPU type, and how many times features were used (for example "3 projects opened, 12 Bench sends"). Never URLs, hosts, traffic, project names, file paths or anything you type. You can turn it off any time in Settings, or with `PLONIX_NO_ANALYTICS=1` or `DO_NOT_TRACK=1`. [docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md) lists exactly what is sent.

## Changes

If these terms change, Plonix shows them again and asks before you continue.
